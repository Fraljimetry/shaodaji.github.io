Tools

Mathematical tools for reading and exploration

Small, focused software for mathematical work. The tools are designed to remain useful without turning the reading or thinking process into a general-purpose productivity interface.

MathReader

A local-first PDF reader built for mathematical papers and notes. Select embedded PDF text, keep reference pages beside the current proof, preserve reading history on your device, and draw a rectangle around a formula to extract LaTeX with in-browser Math OCR.

Design principle

Tools should stay mathematically specific, quiet, and inspectable.

Local first

Keep work on the device

Reader history, notes, cached PDFs, and Math OCR inputs stay local whenever the browser permits it.

Mathematical

Work with formulas and references

The interface is organized around pages, formulas, notation, proof references, and reusable LaTeX rather than generic document editing.
