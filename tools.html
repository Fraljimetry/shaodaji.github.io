Tools

Mathematical tools for reading and illustration

Two independent mathematical tools: MathReader for studying PDFs, and MathIllustrator for constructing and exporting figures. Each stays focused on its own workflow.

Explore MathReader Explore MathIllustrator
Reading · notation · local Math OCR

MathReader

A local-first PDF reader built for mathematical papers and notes. Select embedded PDF text, keep reference pages beside the current proof, preserve reading history on your device, and draw a rectangle around a formula to extract LaTeX with in-browser Math OCR.

Open MathReader
Figures · constructions · diagrams

MathIllustrator

An evolving mathematical illustration editor with live geometric dependencies, programmatic commands, editable vectors and free curves, semantic commutative diagrams, configurable correction, and supported SVG/image/TikZ exports. Advanced 3D and flow visualization are planned, not yet available.

Open MathIllustratorView tutorial

Design principle

Tools should stay mathematically specific, quiet, and inspectable.

Local first

Keep your work in your browser

MathReader keeps reading data locally when storage is available; MathIllustrator works with local projects and editable JSON files. Save exports for durable backups.

Mathematical

From proofs to figures

Read mathematical notation and references in MathReader, or create editable geometric constructions, diagrams, and supported LaTeX exports in MathIllustrator. They are separate applications.
