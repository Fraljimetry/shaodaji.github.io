MathReader

A PDF reader designed for mathematics.

Read locally, keep references beside the current proof, search the paper, and save your place without uploading the PDF to this website.

Selectable PDF text Rectangle formula → LaTeX Pin reference pages Page notes & bookmarks

Your PDF stays on this device. MathReader caches opened PDFs and their reading sessions locally so the last document can reopen where you left it. Formula rectangles use local Image OCR by default, with PDF vector extraction available as a fast fallback. Formula images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to this website.
