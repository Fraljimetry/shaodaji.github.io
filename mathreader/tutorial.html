MathReader · v362

User guide使用指南

A practical guide to reading mathematical PDFs, extracting formulas and connecting papers. The reader stays open in its original tab.

介绍数学 PDF 阅读、公式提取和跨论文关联等操作。指南在新标签页打开，原阅读标签页保持不变。

1. Getting started1. 开始阅读

  1. On the welcome screen, choose Open PDF; or press O (also Ctrl/Cmd + O) anywhere outside a text field.
  2. You can drag a PDF into the reader window on desktop.
  3. After loading a paper, use O again to open another. On a phone or touch-only tablet, open Contents → History → Local library → Open PDF.
  4. Previously cached papers appear in History and on the welcome screen when available.
  1. 在欢迎页点击打开 PDF，或在非输入状态按 O（也支持 Ctrl/Cmd + O）。
  2. 在桌面电脑上，也可以将 PDF 拖入阅读窗口。
  3. 阅读中再次按 O 打开其他论文。无物理键盘的手机或平板使用目录 → 历史 → 本地资料库 → 打开 PDF。
  4. 已缓存的论文可在历史以及欢迎页的最近阅读中重新打开。

The large ? in the top-right header opens this guide. It is not the PDF-open command.

顶部醒目的 ? 用于打开本指南，不是 PDF 打开按钮。

2. Reader controls2. 阅读界面

The main toolbar contains previous/next page arrows, an editable page number, zoom −/+, and a Fit width / Fit page toggle. Use arrow keys or Page Up/Down to navigate when you are not typing.

主工具栏有上一页/下一页、可输入的页码、缩放 −/+ 和适合宽度 / 适合整页切换。非输入状态可用方向键或 Page Up/Down 阅读。

The left sidebar holds Contents, Search, Bookmarks, History; the right workspace holds notes, Formula OCR, metadata, display settings and other reading tools. Both sidebars can be collapsed.

左侧栏含目录、搜索、书签、历史；右侧工作区含页注、公式 OCR、文档信息、显示设置等工具。两侧栏均可折叠。

3. Mathematical indexes3. 数学索引

Open Contents with C. Its four sections are Structure (sections), Results (theorems, lemmas, definitions and proofs), Equations (detected equation labels) and Bibliography (references). Expand a section or use its filter; select an entry to reach its location.

按 C 打开目录。其中有四个区域：结构（章节）、结果（定理、引理、定义及证明）、公式（检测到的公式编号）、参考文献（文献条目）。展开分组或使用筛选，点击条目定位。

Detection is conservative and may miss items, particularly in damaged scans. A displayed index is not a guarantee of complete recognition. P0 is an offline quality/regression program, not a hidden full-document verification while reading.

索引采用保守识别策略，特别是质量较差的扫描件可能漏检。出现索引不代表整篇内容已被完整核对。P0 是离线质量与回归测试体系，不会在阅读时后台自动做全文验证。

4. Link two papers · P14. 关联两篇论文 · P1

P1 connects a bibliography entry in Paper A to the exact PDF of Paper B that you have already opened and cached locally. It never guesses a matching document.

P1 将论文 A 的一个参考文献条目关联到已在本地打开并缓存的论文 B 的确切 PDF，不会猜测目标文档。

  1. Open and cache both papers at least once. Return to Paper A.
  2. Open Contents → Bibliography and locate the entry that cites Paper B.
  3. Right-click the bibliography row and choose Link PDF. On touch, long-press the row. With keyboard focus on it, press Shift + F10 or the Menu key.
  4. Select the exact Paper B from the local-PDF picker and confirm Link.
  5. Right-click the same entry a second time; now choose Open PDF.
  6. Read Paper B, then click ↩ Paper to return to the original location in Paper A. The temporary return path also supports nested jumps.
  1. 分别打开并缓存两篇论文，再返回论文 A。
  2. 打开目录 → 参考文献，找到引用论文 B 的条目。
  3. 右键单击该参考文献行，选择关联 PDF。触屏设备可长按；键盘聚焦后可按 Shift + F10 或菜单键。
  4. 在本地 PDF 选择框中选中确切的论文 B，然后确认关联。
  5. 再次右键单击同一条目，此时选择打开 PDF。
  6. 在论文 B 中阅读后点击↩ 原文，返回论文 A 的原位置。临时返回路径支持多层跳转。

Important: The PDF-link actions appear in the right-click menu, not as permanent buttons beside references. DOI/arXiv links retain their ordinary behavior. P1 opens the confirmed paper; it does not automatically identify a corresponding theorem inside it.

注意：关联操作只在参考文献的右键菜单中出现，不常驻于条目旁。DOI/arXiv 链接保持原有行为。P1 打开确切的论文，但不会自动猜测该论文中的对应定理。

To correct a mapping, right-click and use Relink PDF or Unlink. Confirmed links persist in the browser's local database; the temporary ↩ Paper stack does not survive a reload.

如需修改关联，右键选择重新关联 PDF或取消关联。已确认的关联保存在浏览器本地数据库；临时 ↩ 原文 返回栈在重新加载后不会保留。

5. Extract a formula as LaTeX5. 将公式提取为 LaTeX

  1. Open a PDF, press F to enable formula selection, and drag a rectangle around a formula.
  2. MathReader uses Image OCR by default. On the first image extraction, the Texify model may need to initialize. PDF vector is an alternative for a PDF with a usable text layer.
  3. Inspect the image crop, rendered math and editable LaTeX in the Formula OCR workspace. Correct incorrect symbols before copying or saving an equation.
  4. Press F again or Esc to exit selection mode.
  1. 打开 PDF，按 F 开启公式选取，在公式周围拖出矩形框。
  2. 默认使用图像 OCR。首次从图片提取时，Texify 模型可能需要初始化。对有可用文本层的 PDF，可改用PDF 矢量作为对照。
  3. 在右侧公式 OCR 中核对裁图、渲染结果和可编辑的 LaTeX；复制或收藏前应修正错误符号。
  4. 再按 F 或 Esc 退出选取模式。

A formula that renders successfully can still be mathematically misrecognized. OCR engines can be changed under Settings → Formula OCR; WebGPU may fall back to CPU if needed. Reset OCR unloads the engine until the next image extraction.

成功渲染的公式仍可能存在识别错误。可在设置 → 公式 OCR切换引擎；必要时 WebGPU 会回退到 CPU。重置 OCR 会卸载模型，下次图像提取时再加载。

6. Scanned PDFs and document OCR6. 扫描版 PDF 与全文 OCR

When a scanned PDF has no usable text layer, MathReader may offer Run OCR or Open without OCR. Full-document OCR takes extra time but can enable text-dependent Contents and search. Opening without it keeps the page readable but may leave some indexes unavailable. You can later run OCR from the Contents panel when prompted.

如果扫描版 PDF 没有可用文本层，MathReader 可能提示运行 OCR或不运行 OCR 直接打开。全文 OCR 耗时较长，但可为目录与搜索提供文本。跳过 OCR 仍能看页面，但部分索引可能不可用；之后可根据目录面板提示再运行 OCR。

Document OCR and rectangle Formula OCR are separate workflows. Neither guarantees fully correct handwritten mathematics.

全文 OCR 与矩形公式 OCR 是不同流程。两者都不能保证完整正确识别手写数学内容。

7. Search and command palette7. 搜索和命令面板

Use Ctrl/Cmd + F to search the current PDF. Press Ctrl/Cmd + K for the command palette; type a command such as Open PDF, Bibliography or Rebuild mathematical structure index. MathReader also exposes known mathematical entries through the palette.

用 Ctrl/Cmd + F 搜索当前 PDF。按 Ctrl/Cmd + K 打开命令面板，可输入打开 PDF、参考文献或重新生成数学结构索引等命令。命令面板也可检索已识别的数学条目。

Searching does not silently OCR every formula in every page. Image-only PDFs need document OCR for text search, and unrecognized mathematical expressions may not be searchable.

搜索不会暗中对每页公式做完整 OCR。纯图片 PDF 的文本搜索需要先运行全文 OCR；未识别的数学表达式可能无法搜到。

8. Bookmarks, notes and pinned references8. 书签、批注与固定参考页

Select text to reveal actions such as Copy text, Highlight, Margin note, Page note and Glossary. Use N for the current page note, S for Bookmarks, and P to pin the current PDF page alongside your reading pane. A pin is within the current PDF, not a P1 link to another paper.

选中文字后可使用复制文本、高亮、页边注、页注、符号表等操作。按 N 打开当前页页注，按 S 打开书签，按 P 将当前 PDF 页固定在阅读区旁。固定参考页属于当前 PDF，并不是 P1 跨论文关联。

Use Alt + ← to go back in the within-paper reading trail. The P1 ↩ Paper button is specifically for returning across different PDFs.

按 Alt + ← 可返回文档内的阅读轨迹上一位置；P1 的↩ 原文专门用于跨 PDF 返回。

9. Local library, exports and privacy9. 本地资料库、导出与隐私

Open History for cached local PDFs and Open PDF on touch devices. MathReader keeps paper bytes and reading state in browser storage when caching is available. Export reading data exports reading metadata, not the original PDF files; P1's local link mappings are currently excluded from that export.

在历史中查看已缓存的本地 PDF；触屏设备也可从这里选择打开 PDF。在缓存可用时，MathReader 将 PDF 和阅读状态保存在浏览器存储中。导出阅读数据导出的是阅读元数据，不含原 PDF 文件；目前 P1 的本地关联映射也不包含在该导出中。

Clearing site storage or using a different browser/device can remove the cached papers and links. Keep original PDFs and independent backups. OCR assets may need to be downloaded; your PDF/formula crops are not uploaded to the MathReader website.

清理站点存储或换浏览器/设备可能导致缓存论文与关联消失。请保留原始 PDF 与独立备份。OCR 模型资源可能需要下载；你的 PDF 和公式裁图不会上传到 MathReader 网站。

10. Display and mobile layout10. 显示设置与移动端

Press D for display settings: continuous/single-page reading, page tone, brightness, contrast, spacing and shadows. The header switches language and light/dark themes. On narrow screens, the reader becomes full width: use ☰ Contents and ☷ Workspace to open panels and the visible Close × controls to dismiss them.

按 D 进入显示设置，可调整连续/单页阅读、纸张色调、亮度、对比度、间距和阴影。顶栏可切换语言与明暗主题。窄屏时阅读区铺满宽度；使用☰ 目录、☷ 工作区展开面板，再点击关闭 ×收起。

The top-right ? remains visible on phones. Help opens in a separate tab and does not modify the current PDF.

手机上也能看到顶栏 ?。指南会在新标签页打开，不改变当前 PDF。

12. Troubleshooting12. 常见问题

There is no Open button in the header.

Use O, Ctrl/Cmd + K → Open PDF, drag-and-drop, or History → Local library → Open PDF (touch-friendly).

I cannot see “Link PDF” beside a reference.

That is expected from v361 onward. Right-click the reference row, not its DOI/arXiv link. On a phone, long-press the row. To actually open the linked PDF, right-click again after confirming the link.

My linked paper is not listed or cannot open.

Open the exact target PDF once in MathReader and ensure its bytes are cached. If it was forgotten or browser storage was cleared, open it again and relink manually.

Structure or equations are incomplete.

Check whether the PDF has a usable text layer. Use document OCR for scanned material where offered; mathematical detection is intentionally conservative and imperfect.

Formula OCR freezes or fails.

Wait for the first image-model initialization, check Settings → Formula OCR → Troubleshooting → Reset OCR, or try a CPU engine. Vector extraction may be used for comparison when PDF text exists.

I lost annotations or links after clearing storage.

Browser-local data depends on that browser's storage. Maintain separate backups of original PDFs and exported reading data; P1 links are not yet in the export.

顶栏没有“打开 PDF”按钮。

使用 O、Ctrl/Cmd + K → 打开 PDF、拖入文件，或进入 历史 → 本地资料库 → 打开 PDF（适合触屏）。

参考文献旁看不到“关联 PDF”。

从 v361 起这是预期行为。右键单击文献整行，不要右键 DOI/arXiv 链接；手机上可长按。确认关联后，再次右键选择打开 PDF。

找不到关联目标，或目标无法打开。

先在 MathReader 中打开确切的目标 PDF，确认已缓存。如曾忘记该文件或清理站点存储，请重新打开并手动关联。

结构或公式索引不完整。

确认 PDF 是否存在可用文本层。扫描材料可按提示运行全文 OCR；数学索引采用保守检测，无法保证完全无误。

公式 OCR 卡住或失败。

首次使用可能需要初始化模型；可在 设置 → 公式 OCR → 故障处理 中重置 OCR，或改用 CPU 引擎。有文本层的 PDF 可以用矢量提取作对照。

清理浏览器后批注或关联消失。

本地数据依赖浏览器存储。请独立备份原始 PDF 和导出的阅读数据；P1 关联目前不在导出中。
