# MathReader — Regression policy and recent history

This is the stable entry point for MathReader's development and verification record. The archived v414–v405 release entries follow **unchanged** below the current v415 findings. Earlier records were reorganized into the [historical archive](docs/README.md#regression-history) without dropping or silently rewriting their evidence.

## Standing verification requirements

- Verify **Structure, Results, Equations, and Bibliography** together; track false positives, omissions, printed page coordinates, ordering, and reference navigation. Results and Bibliography cannot currently be edited manually.
- Pair document/index regressions with **formula image-to-LaTeX** checks in the same source PDFs. Distinguish real OCR inference and independently checked mathematical truth from source-level, native-text-proxy, or Chromium-shell tests. Successful tests alone do not certify 100% accuracy.
- Preserve conservative equation-label click geometry, reading-position behavior, existing WebGPU-to-CPU fallback, document identity, annotations, and local-first storage. Do not introduce hidden fallback behavior.
- Preserve the accepted limitation: equation-margin OCR evidence may be lost after hard reload (Ahlfors); do not reopen persistence repair without explicit approval.
- Record baseline-failing evidence before corrections, actual validation commands/results, and unresolved or unverified conditions. Never silently recategorize old FAIL/UNVERIFIED findings as PASS.

## Finding older regression records

See [Documentation map](docs/README.md) for OCR, export, audit, and release documents, and the [regression history archive](docs/README.md#regression-history) for older v338–v404 notes. The historical archive preserves the supplied v414 source record in its original order, including later appendices.

# v412 — Mathematical Results detection: joined headings, split baselines, and conservative reference boundaries (2026-11-08, Beijing UTC+08)

**Baseline:** complete v411 MathReader source. **Scope:** conservative, evidence-backed result-declaration recognition; no UI redesign, equation OCR alteration, formula transcription change, or background persistence repair. Results and Bibliography are non-editable, so both false positives and missing printed declarations are treated as serious correctness issues.

**Reproduced defect 1 — historical joined headings:** original 56-page Lee–Parker *Yamabe Problem* PDF stores headings in its native text as `THEOREM5.1...`, `LEMMA3.5...`, `DEFINITION6.2...`, sometimes without a space before the opening sentence. v411 missed many genuinely printed theorem/lemma/definition/corollary declarations. v412 accepts only bounded, heading-shaped uppercase joins and preserves the actual observed label/position. It does not infer any missing number from a numerical sequence. Prose statements like `THEOREM5.1 shows ...` remain rejected.

**Reproduced defect 2 — independent baselines for title and number:** e.g. printed `THEOREM 5.2` on original p23 is represented by separate PDF text spans on slightly different vertical baselines. The PDF.js-shaped text-line builder can split these into separate entries, leaving a bare `THEOREM` line and a `5.2` number fragment. v412 recovers a label only when the uppercase heading and an explicitly printed numeric fragment have very close x/y geometry (tight baseline, same local column, measured native parts). It rejects separated/ambiguous numbers, lower-case prose heading, distant margin and missing native parts. Such independently printed evidence is stronger than a later weak prose-looking mention of the label.

**Reproduced defect 3 — superscript interrupts prose reference:** in Yamabe p40, the sentence ends `... by`, followed by a tiny math superscript line and `Lemma 9.1. If ...`; previous Results detection indexed that **reference** on p40 instead of the printed lemma statement on p39. v412 checks back over at most two tiny superscript-only intervening lines to detect reference grammar and retains the actual lemma on p39. Reference-tail rejection also covers `therefore shows`, `then implies`, and similar narrative phrasing without excluding actual declaration statements.

**Independent original-PDF evidence and measured boundaries:** the 56-page Yamabe native text-line proxy changes 4→48 result entries; a closer **PDF.js-shaped native-span replay using actual production pageTextLines + buildStructureIndex** changes **15→48**. The 48 recognized label/page pairs agree between the two independent text-segmentation paths and with targeted printed-page inspection (see `regression/v412_content_corpus_audit.json` and saved page montage). This is NOT a full 56-page manually adjudicated recall/precision certification and NOT a real browser's PDF.js output. Bibliography in this replay remains **46**. A separate 88-page math paper keeps **62 Results / 183 bibliography** under the closer span replay in both v411 and v412; the earlier crude 5-entry text-line proxy result is an extraction artifact, not evidence of a deployed v411 failure.

**Historical exact-export regression:** full actual MathReader builder replay from six saved PDF.js `detectorInputLines` exports retains every prior Results and Bibliography entry, count and position: Giusti 207/75, Ahlfors 95/66, SPS 7/17, Simon 4/18, Banach 14/9, saved Yamabe 51/46. # v411 — Exact-click Equation Add; prohibit adjacent label capture (2026-11-08, Beijing UTC+08)

**Baseline:** exact v410 assembled source. **User evidence:** screenshot of original Giusti (1984), PDF p20, showing printed `(1.4)` at the left of a display formula and prose `(1.2)` on the preceding line. The original matching PDF's native text extracts `(1.2)` in the prose line, but omits the visible printed `(1.4)` label. v410 `equationLabelNearPagePoint(page,ratio,exclude)` scans up to **0.065 page height** vertically and never compares the click's horizontal coordinate to the candidate tag. On the subsequent Add path, the `applyEquationEdits()` map blindly calls `out.set(label,...)` and replaces an existing label's position. This explains how clicking `(1.4)` could incorrectly relocate `(1.2)`.

**Correction:** propagate both normalized x and y from the actual primary or continuous page surface; only auto-match a candidate whose measured word-level tag bounds contain the pointer, with a strict vertical line/word bound and at most a 0.002 page-fraction edge allowance. Require *fine-grained word/token geometry*; do not trust a whole-line range or a long PDF.js part's proportional character estimate. If the precise tag cannot be identified (including missing PDF text/OCR), request manual label input for the clicked point; never borrow the nearest tag. A duplicate Add (observed, typed, or selected) is rejected with an actionable Reselect message, leaving the pending Add active; imported historical duplicate additions cannot shadow an automatic index entry on replay. Reselect collisions with an existing distinct label are also blocked. Async stale-document/pending-operation completion cannot create a cross-document edit. Preserve existing saved manual records, editing order, navigated ratio, and index schema.

**Red/green evidence:** `tests/manual_equation_click_precision_v411_regression.js` fails on pristine v410 at the original miscapture (x=.085,y=.265 near prose `(1.2)` at x≈.44,y=.229), then passes **21** assertions in v411 including direct-hit acceptance, vertical/horizontal separation, previously saved duplicates, coarse-geometry rejection, and text-selection Add. `tests/manual_equation_click_browser_v411.py` executes actual production pointer event listeners/functions in headless Chromium against synthetic page surfaces and grounded line geometry: **5** DOM scenarios, including primary and continuous clicks, duplicate clicks, and typed duplicates. It is not a deployed browser/real PDF.js-original-PDF end-to-end certification. The existing `manual_appendix_equation_v405_regression.js` retains **38** assertions and now supplies a 2D point and newly extracted duplicate guards.

**Gate:** 56/56 JavaScript suites; 24/24 Python/Chromium suites (slow harnesses verified separately); all JavaScript syntax and assembled module validation; root ZIP CRC and byte-exact clean overlay replay over the exact original v410. Only manual Equation Add/Reselect geometry and persistence collision handling changed, plus PDF.js native `pageTextLines` exposes measured native item heights for correct hit-testing. Structure/Results/Bibliography/automatic equation detection, OCR jobs, canvas rendering, storage schema, and the accepted hard-reload OCR evidence limitation are unchanged. v411 updates only browser asset cache keys.

# v410 — Recover intermittent first-open continuous-page paint failures (2026-11-08, Beijing UTC+08)

**Baseline:** exact v409 source and root-level differential patch. **Symptom:** when first opening a PDF, continuous reading occasionally displays `Continuous reading could not render the current page.` and the active page stays blank until the user changes zoom. The message originates only in the first continuous-document builder, after an initial PDF.js canvas render and a primary-render fallback. The original release treated the 48×48 downsampled canvas brightness-variation heuristic as a hard failure; genuinely white/sparse pages also fail this heuristic, while the normal zoom rebuild skips this verification entirely. A genuinely rejected PDF.js render can also be transient at first open.

**Correction:** keep PDF.js paint success and a non-placeholder canvas as the decisive conditions; treat the 48×48 content sample as diagnostic rather than proof of failure. If the first paint seems blank, retain the existing independent primary-render fallback and then retry the original page at the **same zoom** on a **freshly attached canvas** after two layout frames. Failed continuous PDF.js tasks also receive the bounded fresh-canvas attempt, and only then the independent primary-render fallback. A genuine unrecoverable task failure still reports the existing error. Successful blank or sparse pages no longer prevent neighboring pages from initializing. Retry guards reject stale generation/document/page ownership; reading anchor, sidebar and zoom mode are unchanged. The recovered-primary path now restores selectable text/annotations when the continuous task never supplied overlays.

**Reproduced red/green:** new Playwright/Chromium regression `tests/continuous_initial_repaint_v410.py` executes the production `30-rendering-navigation.js` lifecycle with real DOM/canvas/requestAnimationFrame/observer and mock PDF.js page paints. On unmodified v409 its first case fails with the exact reported error (`first_blank_then_good`, retry never attempted). On v410, 25 assertions pass across initial blank then successful repaint, legitimate all-white page, transient PDF.js rejection, independent primary-only recovery, persistent failure/error, normal no-extra-repaint, and invalidated generation. This simulates paint timing; **not live-site acceptance with actual PDF.js rendering of the user's intermittently failing PDF**. A bounded retry may not solve every browser/GPU issue; residual failures are reported rather than hidden.

**Regression gate:** 55/55 existing JavaScript suites; 23/23 Python/Chromium suites (the two historically stalled harness suites were checked separately); v410 JS/bootstrap syntax and ten-part assembled-runtime syntax; root ZIP CRC and byte-exact replay against the untouched v409 source.
